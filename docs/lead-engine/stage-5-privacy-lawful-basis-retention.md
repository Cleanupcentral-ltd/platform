# Lead Engine Lite Stage 5 — privacy, lawful basis and retention record

Last reviewed: 14 August 2026

## Purpose and scope

This record documents the approved privacy position for Clean Up Central Ltd's founder-only Lead Engine. It should be read with the public Privacy Notice and Website Terms of Use.

The Lead Engine is used to identify and assess organisations that may reasonably need Clean Up Central's cleaning and property-support services, record first-party or appropriate official evidence, prepare founder-reviewed outreach drafts, record genuine contact activity and respect objections or do-not-contact decisions.

It is not an autonomous marketing sender. Outlook access is draft-only and the production workflow must not obtain `Mail.Send` permission.

## Data sources and permitted use

### Google Places

Google Places is discovery-only at the durable database boundary.

The Lead Engine may use current Google Places results transiently to show founders useful discovery information such as the business name, address, website, phone, distance and source link. That transient display does not make Google Places the durable source of verified prospect identity or contact information.

For a Google-discovered prospect, the durable Google-derived identifier is the Google Place ID. Durable identity, sector, contact and qualification evidence must come from first-party websites or an appropriate official source.

The completed Stage 5 storage correction removes previously stored Google-derived business content except the Place ID. The finished user experience must remain useful by retrieving current Place details when needed for display and by progressively replacing discovery-only information with independently verified durable evidence.

### First-party websites

The organisation's own website is the preferred source for:

- trading identity;
- approved-sector evidence;
- current website URL;
- public generic business email;
- public contact form;
- evidence of locations, properties, managed units or recurring operational need;
- labelled company, charity or operator information where appropriate.

Evidence must retain the source URL, relevant supporting text or fact, capture date and verification status.

### Official sources

Appropriate official sources include, depending on organisation type:

- Companies House for companies and LLPs;
- Charity Commission evidence for relevant charities;
- official institutional or public-body sources where Companies House is not the appropriate verifier.

Companies House is not a mandatory verifier for every organisation.

## Direct-marketing eligibility rule

The production email workflow is intentionally narrower than the maximum activity that may be legally possible.

An initial marketing email draft may be prepared only where all of the following are true:

1. the organisation is verified as a corporate subscriber for the approved email workflow;
2. the business identity and approved sector are verified from durable evidence;
3. an active status or equivalent current-operation signal is verified;
4. the recipient is a verified generic business email published by the organisation on its first-party website;
5. the current assessment has no qualification blocker and the prospect is in the ready-for-outreach state;
6. there is no active suppression, objection or do-not-contact record.

Sole traders and unincorporated partnerships are not approved for the automated email-draft route under Lean v1. They remain blocked unless Clean Up Central later adopts and documents a separate compliant basis and workflow.

A prospect score never overrides a legal, verification, suppression or subscriber-type blocker.

## Lawful-basis record

### Public prospect research and qualification

**Purpose:** identify relevant business organisations, avoid irrelevant outreach, verify public business contact routes and maintain evidence for founder decisions.

**Lawful basis where personal data is involved:** legitimate interests.

**Clean Up Central interest:** proportionate business development for professional cleaning and property-support services, coupled with evidence-based targeting that reduces indiscriminate outreach.

**Necessity:** the limited public business information is needed to determine whether an organisation is relevant, active, contactable through an approved business route and safe to approach.

**Balancing controls:** founder-only access; approved sectors; bounded research; first-party/official evidence preference; generic business email requirement; corporate-subscriber gate; no automatic sending; suppression checks; clear privacy information; immediate marketing objection handling; retention limits.

### Initial B2B outreach to approved corporate prospects

**Purpose:** introduce relevant Clean Up Central services to a suitable corporate business prospect.

**Lawful basis where the communication processes personal data:** legitimate interests, subject to the production eligibility controls above and the applicable electronic-marketing rules.

The email must identify Clean Up Central, include a straightforward way to object to further marketing and provide access to the public Privacy Notice.

### Enquiries and quotations

Use the lawful basis appropriate to the actual request, normally steps requested before entering into a contract and/or legitimate interests for responding to and administering the enquiry.

### Customers and service delivery

Use contract where processing is necessary to provide the agreed service, together with legal obligation or legitimate interests where separately applicable to accounting, safety, claims, security or operational records.

### Suppression records

**Purpose:** ensure an objection or do-not-contact decision is respected and prevent accidental future marketing.

**Lawful basis:** legitimate interests and, where applicable, compliance with data-protection/direct-marketing obligations.

Only the minimum information needed to identify the suppression should be retained.

## Transparency

The public Privacy Notice must be live before Google discovery is re-enabled for normal founder use or live outreach begins.

Where Clean Up Central holds identifiable business-contact data obtained from a public source, the individual must be given the required privacy information within the applicable transparency period and, where the data is used to communicate with them, no later than the first communication unless a valid exception applies.

For Lean v1, the approved initial outreach email should provide a clear privacy-notice route so transparency is delivered at first communication.

## Objections and suppression

Any direct-marketing objection, unsubscribe request or explicit request not to be contacted must be treated as an immediate stop signal.

The prospect/email/domain should be suppressed at the appropriate level needed to honour the request. Suppression is not a temporary follow-up status and must not be removed merely because the normal prospect-retention period expires.

A founder may lift a suppression only where there is a documented reason showing the original restriction no longer applies; it must never be lifted simply to retry marketing.

## Retention schedule

These are Clean Up Central operational retention limits, not claims that UK law prescribes these exact periods. Records may be retained longer where a legal claim, statutory obligation, active customer relationship, security investigation or other documented requirement makes that necessary.

| Record category | Default retention | Trigger / action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery-only Google Place ID for a prospect that never obtains durable verification | 12 months | Review at 12 months; delete if no continuing prospecting purpose. If still relevant, refresh the Place ID/details before further use. |
| Excluded, rejected, duplicate or clearly unsuitable prospect research | 6 months | Delete ordinary research/evidence after 6 months unless needed to explain an active suppression, complaint or dispute. |
| Uncontacted verified prospect remaining potentially relevant | 12 months from last verification/research activity | Reverify before further outreach; delete if there is no continuing business-development purpose. |
| Draft prepared but never sent and no meaningful prospect activity follows | 90 days | Delete or minimise the abandoned draft while retaining any independent evidence still within its own retention period. |
| Contacted prospect / meaningful outreach history that does not become a customer | 24 months from last meaningful interaction or closure | Review and delete/minimise after 24 months unless there is a documented continuing relationship, complaint, dispute or legal need. |
| Follow-up activity | Until resolved, then follows the contacted-prospect period | Do not create indefinite follow-ups. Closed follow-up history follows the 24-month outreach period. |
| Suppression / direct-marketing objection | Retain the minimum suppression record for as long as needed to prevent renewed marketing | Periodically review for accuracy; do not delete merely because ordinary prospect records expire. |
| Search/export audit records | 12 months | Retain long enough to investigate access, export and compliance events; then delete/minimise unless an incident requires longer retention. |
| Expired or consumed Outlook OAuth state | 30 days maximum | Delete after expiry/consumption and troubleshooting window. |
| Outlook connection tokens | While the founder connection is active | Delete promptly when connection is revoked or no longer required. |

## Data minimisation rules

- Do not collect personal contact data merely because it is publicly available.
- Prefer generic organisational addresses such as `info@`, `hello@`, `bookings@`, `facilities@` or equivalent approved business routes.
- Do not enrich prospects with private social profiles, sensitive personal information or unrelated information about staff.
- Do not retain copied Google Places content as durable prospect evidence where the applicable Google Maps Platform terms do not permit it.
- Do not keep evidence that no longer supports an active operational, legal, suppression or audit purpose.

## Security and access

The Lead Engine remains founders-only. Production access requires the approved authentication/MFA controls. Lead Engine tables are not exposed for ordinary public/anonymous use, and sensitive integration credentials must remain server-side.

CSV exports are a controlled founder fallback. Exports must be treated as confidential operational data, stored only where needed for the drill or genuine fallback, and deleted when no longer required.

## Review triggers

Review this record before any of the following:

- automatic or bulk email sending;
- outreach to sole traders or unincorporated partnerships;
- use of named individual business emails as a normal outreach route;
- addition of new lead-data providers;
- use of non-public or purchased contact datasets;
- material changes to Google Maps Platform terms;
- material changes to UK direct-marketing/data-protection law or ICO guidance;
- expansion of the Lead Engine beyond founder-only use.
