Visual direction

Premium but accessible, warm, refined and highly legible. Use white and ivory surfaces, soft beige photography, black editorial type and restrained gold accents. Pages should feel compact and deliberate: fewer sections, strong horizontal rules, clean split layouts and minimal card repetition. Avoid dark luxury templates, gradients, glassmorphism, oversized rounded cards, floating blobs and decorative clutter.
